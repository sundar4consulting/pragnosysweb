The name is the method.

Pragnosys joins three ideas. Read the highlighted letters down the page.

Prajna

Prajna is Sanskrit for discerning wisdom: seeing a situation as it is, before the story told about it.

Gnosis

Gnosis is Greek for knowing that comes from direct experience and evidence, not hearsay.

System

System is structure that lets knowing repeat: workflows, models, and habits that keep working when you leave the room.

Wisdom without structure stays an opinion. Structure without wisdom automates the wrong thing.

    From a messy question to a running system.

    Each engagement moves from the amber side of the page to the turquoise side, in this order.

    1. 1

      Observe

      Listen for what is actually happening, not the summary of it.

    2. 2

      Reduce

      Strip the problem to its facts and constraints.

    3. 3

      Model

      Make the logic explicit enough to test and challenge.

    4. 4

      Build

      Turn the model into workflows, agents, and decision tools.

    5. 5

      Operate

      Hand over something your team can run, question, and improve.
